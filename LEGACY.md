# Sley 1.x Legacy Lineage

Sley 1.x is complete and frozen. This repository preserves the human-readable
structural language, its implementation, releases, evidence, and historical
ecosystem for existing users, compatibility work, study, experimentation, and
forks.

Active Sley development continues in the separate, intentionally incompatible
machine-native Sley 2.x lineage. Sley 2 is developed in the open at
<https://github.com/sley-lang/sley> and documented at <https://sleylang.org/docs>.

Sley 2 does not inherit the Sley 1.x textual syntax, compiler, conventional
LSP, Tree-sitter grammar, or compatibility tooling. Those components remain
historical Sley 1.x material in this repository.

No license was changed by the consolidation. See [LICENSE_SCOPE.md](LICENSE_SCOPE.md)
for the component boundaries and [MIGRATION_MAP.md](MIGRATION_MAP.md) for source
provenance.
